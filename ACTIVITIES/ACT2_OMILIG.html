My Favorite Poem



I sit upon the white rocks by the bay,
Against whose hollows numberless,
the waves Will splash forevermore.
The sunset laves The deep with light.
There rise far awayA mountain that has seemingly defied
The very clouds unchanged. And near, portending,
That all created things will have an ending.

By: Fernando M. Maramág
