About Marv's Dental

We are a family dental clinic focused on prevention first, treatment second. Every visit starts with a conversation, not a drill.

History

  1. Clinic opens
    Marv's Dental starts with one chair and a promise to explain every step to the patient.
  2. Team grows
    A hygienist and front-desk staff join, and we add braces and whitening.
  3. Community care
    We begin school brushing drills and free check-up days.
  4. Online reservations
    Patients can now book from home, any time.
